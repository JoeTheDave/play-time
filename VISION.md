# PlayTime — Vision

## Purpose
PlayTime is a browser-based per-player turn timer for board game sessions. It solves the problem of tracking each player's cumulative time investment and current turn duration without requiring a dedicated device or app — open a URL, type in player names, and start timing.

## Target User
Board game players — casual families and hobbyist gamers alike — who want to track how long each player spends on their turns without the friction of a dedicated physical clock or a heavyweight companion app.

## Value Proposition
A chess clock for any number of players (2–12), running in any browser with no installation. Unlike phone timers or chess clocks (two-player only), PlayTime gives every player their own live tile showing real-time total time, current turn duration, and recent turn history at a glance.

## Business Model
Personal hobby project — no revenue model. Free to use.

## Competitive Landscape
- **Chess clock apps / physical clocks**: two-player only, not browser-based
- **Generic timer apps**: single countdown, no per-player tracking or history
- **Board game companion apps**: heavier, often game-specific, require installation

PlayTime differentiates on simplicity and multi-player support: no install, no account, just a URL.
